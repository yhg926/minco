# Artifacts

- `NOTE.md`: experiment interpretation and result table.
- `commands.sh`: reproducible profiling and scoring commands.
- `score_cami3_coden15_formula_af.py`: scorer for coden15 original and formula-AF gates.
- `summary.tsv`: combined mean CAMI3 result table including Sylph and old ctx-marker.
- `coden15_cami3_mean_abundance_renorm.tsv`: coden15 mean abundance metrics using renormalized predictions.
- `coden15_cami3_mean_presence.tsv`: coden15 mean presence metrics.
- `coden15_cami3_sample_abundance.tsv`: per-sample coden15 abundance metrics.
- `coden15_cami3_sample_presence.tsv`: per-sample coden15 presence metrics.
- `coden15_cami3_selected_species.tsv`: selected species after gate plus rescue.
- `coden15_cami3_rescued_species.tsv`: audit of intra-genus rescue additions.
- `cami3_sample*_coden15_ctxmarker_split_naive_product.tsv`: raw coden15 MinCO readwise profile outputs.
- `logs/`: MinCO stderr/stdout and `/usr/bin/time -v` logs.
