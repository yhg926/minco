# Artifacts

- `NOTE.md`: interpretation and headline tables.
- `commands.sh`: reproducible compile and score commands.
- `score_gtdb_ani_accuracy.py`: scorer for GTDB-mapped ANI accuracy.
- `gtdb_truth_transfer_audit.tsv`: per-NCBI-species GTDB transfer status and mapped GTDB species.
- `gtdb_truth_transfer_summary.tsv`: per-sample unique, ambiguous, and unmapped truth counts.
- `selected_call_gtdb_ani_metrics.tsv`: per-sample selected-call ANI metrics.
- `selected_call_gtdb_ani_mean_metrics.tsv`: mean selected-call ANI metrics.
- `selected_call_gtdb_ani_details.tsv`: selected GTDB species calls and truth compatibility flags.
- `candidate_gtdb_ani95_metrics.tsv`: per-sample candidate ANI>=0.95 metrics.
- `candidate_gtdb_ani95_mean_metrics.tsv`: mean candidate ANI>=0.95 metrics.
- `candidate_gtdb_ani95_details.tsv`: raw candidate species collapsed by GTDB species.
